# Level design handoff

Abyss floor 11 opens from the Root Antechamber after the saved boss defeat. The dry path and the marsh converge before the root arch. The tracked Stalker defeat must save before the exit opens. Structural checks for 80 walkable cells, alternate approach connectivity and a gated exit pass with `python3 -B scripts/create_authored_campaign_maps.py --validate`.

Floor 12 is a continuous incline with a protected, optional overlook. Its stair records traversal after floor 11, then opens floor 13 when that package exists. Tests passed for 95 walkable cells and the traversal receipt.

Floor 13 provides two approaches to the upper terrace, with an optional Stalker on the short route. Its release control opens both the gated descent and the return shortcut after the checkpoint saves. Structural checks passed for 140 walkable cells, bypass clearance and the gated switchback.

Floor 14 uses two drains that save independently. The second drain commits the final water drop, opens the exit and records completion. Structural validation passed for 102 cells and a raised service route outside the ground Stalker leash. The handwheel and cues are available as prototypes.

Floor 15 uses a single pin and two carrying routes. The pin can be dropped and retrieved and returns to the alcove if lost. The walkway is enabled after successful seating and checkpoint. Offline validation covers the unsupported gap, exit gate and two carrying routes.

Floor 16 uses two walkable canal inlets and an inner-side counterweight. The release remains locked from the wrong side of the portcullis. The gate and weight move only after a successful checkpoint.

Floor 17 has two separated Stalker sectors and a stable island. The bridge aligns once with the far bank after checking the rotational path is empty and saving. Verify bridge and gate geometry before and after reloading. Floor 18 remains pending.

Generate `AbyssFloor011` through `AbyssFloor017` with the campaign generator and run the [playtest](../first-playtest.md#buried-forest-threshold) in UE 5.6. The environment meshes, creature and audio await import and runtime testing.
