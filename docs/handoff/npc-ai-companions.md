## Integration contracts proposed
Campaign owns canonical flags and chapter gates; it must authorize recruitment/departure, never let AI change ending eligibility. Use stable character IDs and separate reality-instance IDs for alternate versions. Chapter 2 introduces Noah; chapter 22 removes him from current reality, with Kael alone remembering. Do not infer supernatural knowledge from combat role. Campaign save must own a transactional snapshot of NPC state; current policy is in-memory only, not a second save slot. Coordinate relationship authority with existing campaign Relationships map.
Combat supplies alive/health, hostility, range and action availability; AI emits intent, never applies damage directly. Core supplies streamed actors, time, lifecycle and navigation. Narrative supplies localized dialogue IDs and authorized transitions. Discrepancy supplies monotonically increasing revision and per-character retention rules; apply multiple memory updates at same revision per distinct key. AI state must not leak discarded memories into dialogue.
