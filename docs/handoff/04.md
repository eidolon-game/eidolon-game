# AI handoff

The Root Stalker at Abyss floor 11 uses a visibility trace, remembers the last seen position, and returns to its ambush after searching. It telegraphs a short root sweep or a pounce with a fixed direction. The inherited defeat delegate feeds the authored encounter checkpoint.

`tests/forest_stalker_attack.cpp` covers sight memory, search expiry, pounce lock, sweep evasion and recovery. It passed as a standalone C++17 executable. Check native collision, enemy movement, damage and warning readability in UE 5.6 during the [floor 11 playtest](../first-playtest.md#buried-forest-threshold).

Floor 13 adds an optional climb and fixed landing using the authored anchors. A blocked sweep reverses the path, and only a confirmed landing can deal area damage. The ground behavior remains available if the anchors are absent or unusable. The standalone test now covers trajectory bounds, reversal and the landing radius. Verify the climb and impact with Unreal collision and the authored terrace during the [floor 13 playtest](../first-playtest.md#branch-terraces).

The floor 16 Stalker follows ramp grades by sampling and sweeping to stable ground. Its pounce stays horizontal and cancels when the locked path loses suitable support. The standalone tests passed for legal slope steps, cliffs and flat pounce tolerance. Test both openings and evasive routes in the [Rootlet Canal playtest](../first-playtest.md#rootlet-canal).

Floor 18 pairs the two optional Stalkers. Each checks its partner before starting a ground or perch attack. A valid elevated Stalker waits for an initial target at its perch, and the surviving partner can attack after a recovery, aborted drop or defeat. Verify overlap rejection, visibility and fallback in the [Double Clearing playtest](../first-playtest.md#double-clearing).

Riftroot Tangle on floor 20 alternates an aimed rock wedge and a one-sided root draw. Its 45% relocation waits for recovery, checks the center path and performs no attack. The standalone test covers timings, bounded volumes, transition ordering and completion. Verify collision, telegraphs and defeat checkpoint in the [floor 20 playtest](../first-playtest.md#roots-in-the-fissure).

The optional Mine Borer on floor 21 approaches, lowers its digging head and charges along the authored axis. The adapter sweeps movement and checks cover for debris. Rear hits gain damage during recovery. `tests/mine_borer_attack.cpp` checks charge distance, debris bounds and the rear window. Confirm collision and visuals in the [floor 21 playtest](../first-playtest.md#quarry-threshold).
